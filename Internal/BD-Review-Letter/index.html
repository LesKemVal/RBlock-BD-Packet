R. Block Nation — Broker-Dealer Review Letter

Prepared for Broker-Dealer Review (Andes Capital)
Last Updated: December 2025

This letter is provided to support broker-dealer review of the current R. Block Nation platform architecture, compliance posture, and operational readiness. It is intended to accompany the materials contained within this BD & Compliance Packet.

1. Platform Status (Current)

R. Block Nation is an early-stage, compliance-first digital asset and investment infrastructure platform designed to support JOBS Act offerings, beginning with Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF), with an architectural path toward Regulation A+ and Regulation D.

The platform is presently in a pre-live, broker-dealer review phase. No live offerings are publicly solicited or transacted without broker-dealer oversight, escrow, and third-party compliance services.

2. Compliance Architecture Overview

R. Block Nation has been designed to intentionally separate regulated functions from proprietary platform logic. Regulated services are delegated to established third-party providers and broker-dealer partners.

  • KYC / AML and investor verification handled by third-party compliance vendors
  • Escrow, payment processing, and funds custody handled by regulated providers
  • No internal custody of investor funds or assets
  • No internal broker-dealer representation or solicitation activities

The platform’s role is limited to orchestration, recordkeeping, and compliant investor experience flows, subject to broker-dealer review and approval.

3. Smart Contract & Tokenization Posture

Smart contracts are utilized strictly for post-issuance recordkeeping, governance signaling, and cap-table integrity — not for custody, trading, or unregulated transfer.

  • Transfer restrictions enforced at the contract level
  • Investor verification gates prior to any token issuance
  • Upgradeable architecture to accommodate BD-mandated changes
  • No secondary trading enabled without explicit regulatory approval

4. Auditability & Oversight

The platform maintains structured, immutable audit logs capturing investor actions, document views, acknowledgements, and system events.

These logs are designed to support broker-dealer supervision, regulatory examination, and issuer reporting obligations.

5. Intended Broker-Dealer Relationship

R. Block Nation does not seek to replace or bypass broker-dealer functions. The intended model is a supervised platform relationship where:

  • The broker-dealer retains supervisory authority
  • Offering eligibility and investor access are BD-approved
  • All regulated activities flow through BD-approved vendors
  • The platform remains adaptable to BD compliance requirements

6. Current Ask

The purpose of this review is to:

  • Confirm architectural alignment with broker-dealer expectations
  • Identify required changes prior to any live deployment
  • Establish a compliant path forward under BD supervision

No public offering will proceed without broker-dealer approval, escrow integration, and finalized compliance workflows.
