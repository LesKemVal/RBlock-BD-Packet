R. Block Nation — Platform Status Letter

Prepared for Broker-Dealer Review
Date: December 2025

This letter is provided to summarize the current operational and technical status of the R. Block Nation platform for broker-dealer review and alignment.

1. Platform Purpose

R. Block Nation is a JOBS Act–compliant digital platform designed to support Regulation CF, Regulation A+, and Regulation D offerings using a modular, broker-dealer-supervised architecture.

2. Current Platform Status

  • Investor onboarding flows implemented and auditable
  • Issuer onboarding and document submission flows implemented
  • Document review, acknowledgement, and audit logging in place
  • Commitment and post-commit investor flows implemented
  • All compliance-relevant actions logged via internal audit trail

3. Compliance & Oversight Model

The platform is designed to operate under broker-dealer supervision. No securities are issued, accepted, or finalized without broker-dealer review and approval.

  • All investor actions are logged with timestamps
  • No self-custody of investor funds
  • No investment advice or recommendations presented
  • Offering materials are issuer-provided and review-gated

4. Technology & Audit Readiness

The system maintains a deterministic audit trail for:

  • Document views
  • Investment intent and commitment steps
  • Issuer submissions
  • Post-commit actions

All logs are reviewable and exportable for broker-dealer and regulatory use.

5. Current Review Status

The platform is currently in a broker-dealer pre-review and alignment phase. No public fundraising activity is active or live.

This document reflects the current state of the platform as of the date above.

R. Block Nation
